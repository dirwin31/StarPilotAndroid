### Continuity handoff

Independent continuity authority activates only through either:

1. **Written handoff.** The Primary Maintainer expressly authorizes a handoff
   in an attributable written statement identifying its scope and effective
   date. That statement determines whether the handoff is temporary or permanent.
2. **Inactivity and unanswered contact.** All of the following conditions are met:
   - The Primary Maintainer has performed no substantive project maintenance
     for at least six consecutive calendar months. Code changes, reviews,
     release decisions, issue responses, and project-management communications
     count as activity, including private communications known to the Team.
     An attributable confirmation to the Team that the app remains supported,
     that no update is currently necessary, or that the Primary Maintainer
     remains available to address support needs also counts as activity and
     restarts the six-month period. No minimum commit or release frequency is
     required. A lack of code changes or new releases alone is not abandonment.
     Unrelated online activity and automated actions do not count.
   - There is a documented, concrete need to maintain, support, or continue
     distributing the official app, such as a reproducible defect, security
     issue, platform compatibility problem, store requirement, or loss of an
     official distribution channel. A desire for optional features, a different
     design, or more frequent releases alone is insufficient. If the app is
     operating as intended and no such need exists, inactivity alone does not
     activate continuity authority.
   - After those six months, Firestar5683 or a duly designated Team lead sends
     three good-faith direct messages to the Primary Maintainer using their
     most recently designated contact channel, or an established DM channel
     previously used successfully to communicate with them. If multiple such
     channels are known, use at least two across the three attempts.
   - The attempts are at least 15 days apart, spanning at least 30 days. Each
     message requests a response and clearly states that the Team intends to
     activate this continuity provision if no response is received, identifies
     the concrete need, and describes the proposed action. Failed or
     bounced delivery attempts do not count; a read receipt is not required.
     Public posts, issue comments, and messages to unrelated accounts do not
     substitute for these DMs.
   - At least 14 days pass after the third attempt without any attributable
     reply from the Primary Maintainer to the Team, and without resumed
     substantive maintenance. Any reply, including a refusal or request for
     more time, stops this activation process. A new process requires a fresh
     six-month period starting from that reply or subsequent project activity.
   - The Team lead records the last known activity, contact dates and channels,
     concrete need, absence of a reply, and activation date, and sends an activation notice
     through the same DM channels. Retain evidence privately; publish only a
     concise status notice, without private messages or contact details.

Once validly activated, the Team may maintain, update, and distribute the
official app within section 2 without further approval while the handoff is
in effect. If the Primary Maintainer returns after inactivity activation,
